Volunteer Roles  ·  स्वयंसेवक भूमिका

Find Your Role

तुमची भूमिका शोधा
🚗
Collection Driver
संकलन चालक

Drive our collection vehicle or your own transport to pick up donated waste from households across Nagpur. Weekends or weekday mornings welcome.

🔨
Upcycling Artisan
कारागीर स्वयंसेवक

Use your carpentry, tailoring, painting, or craft skills to help transform collected waste into useful, beautiful products at our workshop.

📣
Community Ambassador
समुदाय दूत

Spread awareness in your mohalla, society, or workplace. Organise collection drives, recruit donors, and bring more of Nagpur into this movement.

📱
Digital Volunteer
डिजिटल स्वयंसेवक

Help us grow online — manage social media, create content, photograph our work, or help with website updates. Remote-friendly role.

🎁
Distribution Helper
वितरण सहाय्यक

Join our distribution drives and help deliver upcycled products to families, shelters, and community centres across Nagpur with care and dignity.

📚
Skills Trainer
कौशल्य प्रशिक्षक

Teach upcycling skills — woodwork, stitching, painting, basic repair — to underprivileged youth and adults in our workshop programme.

What to Donate  ·  काय दान करावे

Your Unwanted Is Someone's Needed

तुमचे नको ते कोणाचे हवे ते
🪑
Furniture
फर्निचर

Chairs, tables, beds, shelves, almirahs — broken or old, we will repair and repurpose them.

👕
Clothing & Fabric
कपडे आणि कापड

Old clothes, bedsheets, curtains, fabric scraps — cleaned and converted into quilts, bags, and more.

📺
Electronics
इलेक्ट्रॉनिक्स

Old fans, lamps, radios, small appliances — many can be repaired and given a second life.

🍳
Utensils & Kitchenware
भांडी

Pots, pans, plates, cups, steel items — cleaned and distributed to families who need them.

🧸
Toys & Books
खेळणी आणि पुस्तके

Children's books, toys, and educational material — given to shelters, schools, and children in need.

🔩
Metal & Scrap
धातू आणि स्क्रॅप

Pipes, rods, wire, sheet metal — upcycled by our artisans into tools and useful hardware.
