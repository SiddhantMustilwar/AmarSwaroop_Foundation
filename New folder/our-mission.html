What We Do  ·  आम्ही काय करतो

A Mission Built on Three Pillars

तीन स्तंभांवर उभे ध्येय
🚪
Collect
संकलन करणे

We go door to door across Nagpur's neighbourhoods collecting discarded materials — furniture, electronics, fabric, metal, plastic — that would otherwise end up in landfills.

🛠️
Transform
रूपांतरण करणे

Skilled artisans and upcycling workshops breathe new life into collected waste — turning broken chairs into furniture, old fabric into quilts, scrap into tools.

🎁
Give with Dignity
सन्मानाने देणे

Every finished product is given free of cost to underprivileged families, shelters, and elderly individuals — because dignity is not a luxury, it is a right.

"कचऱ्यातून निर्माण होतो सन्मान, आणि सन्मानातून घडतो समाज."

"From waste is born dignity, and from dignity is built society."

Why It Matters  ·  हे का महत्त्वाचे आहे

The Problem We Solve

आम्ही सोडवतो ती समस्या

Nagpur generates thousands of tonnes of solid waste every year. A significant portion of this is not trash — it is discarded household goods, wearable clothing, and functional materials that families simply no longer need.

At the same time, a large portion of Nagpur's population lives without access to basic furnishings, utensils, and household items. These two realities exist just streets apart — and nobody was connecting them.

That is the gap Amar Swaroop Foundation was built to close. We are not a charity that gives handouts — we are a circular system that gives dignity through resourcefulness.

"जेव्हा एकाचा कचरा दुसऱ्याची गरज भागवतो, तेव्हा समाज खऱ्या अर्थाने श्रीमंत होतो."

"When one person's waste fulfils another's need, society becomes truly wealthy."

How It Works  ·  हे कसे काम करते

The Process

प्रक्रिया
१

Waste Collection Request

कचरा संकलनाची विनंती

Residents of Nagpur fill our Waste Collection Form to schedule a free door-to-door pickup at their convenience.

२

Volunteer Pickup

स्वयंसेवकांकडून संकलन

Our trained volunteers collect the materials, sort them on-site, and transport them to our upcycling centre.

३

Sorting & Assessment

वर्गीकरण आणि मूल्यांकन

Every item is assessed — what can be directly donated, what needs repair, and what can be upcycled into something new.

४

Upcycling & Repair

पुनर्निर्मिती आणि दुरुस्ती

Skilled artisans repair, clean, and upcycle materials into finished products — furniture, clothing, kitchenware, and more.

५

Distribution to Families

कुटुंबांना वितरण

Products are delivered free of cost to verified underprivileged families, shelters, and community centres across Nagpur.
