Chapter One  ·  सुरुवात

It Began With a Single Walk

एका फेरीपासून सुरुवात झाली

In the narrow lanes of Nagpur, where the morning sun turns the old city walls golden, a young man named Manoj Band was walking through his neighbourhood in 2017. It was an ordinary morning — except for what he saw. Heaped outside every third door were piles of discarded objects: a broken chair, tangled wires, worn-out clothes, rusted utensils. Things that people had given up on.

But a few streets away, Manoj had seen something else — a family sitting on the floor because they had no furniture. Children doing homework in dim light because they could not afford a proper lamp. The contrast lodged itself deep in his heart and would not let go.

That evening, he asked himself a question that would change his life: "जर हे कचरा त्यांच्यापर्यंत पोहोचवता आले तर?" — "What if this waste could reach the ones who need it most?"

🌱

Manoj Band

मनोज बंड

Founder, Amar Swaroop Foundation

The Founder  ·  संस्थापक

A Heart That Refused
to Look Away

एक मन जे दुर्लक्ष करू शकले नाही

Manoj Band grew up in Nagpur — Maharashtra's Orange City — deeply aware of the inequalities within its streets. He witnessed how much perfectly usable material was discarded every single day, while just around the corner, families lived without basic necessities.

He was not an entrepreneur by profession, nor a social worker by training. He was simply a person who cared — deeply, stubbornly, relentlessly. In 2017, with no funding, no office, and just a handful of willing friends, he started going door to door.

"मी एकट्याने सुरू केलं, पण हे काम कधीच माझं एकट्याचं नव्हतं — हे नागपूरच्या प्रत्येक माणसाचं आहे." "I started alone, but this work was never mine alone — it belongs to every person of Nagpur."

Today, Manoj continues to lead every initiative personally — from planning collection drives to overseeing upcycling workshops — driven by the same conviction he had on that very first walk in 2017.

"कचरा म्हणजे फक्त टाकाऊ वस्तू नाही — तो एक संधी आहे."

"Waste is not just something discarded — it is an opportunity."

— Manoj Band  ·  संस्थापक, Amar Swaroop Foundation
